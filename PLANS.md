# 11. Live reading checklist

For **TAQA / KORA / CRST / RUBX / MAAL / NAPR / ACTF**, inspect in this order:

1. **Where is price?** At a mapped support/resistance level or in the middle of nowhere?
2. **Spread:** tight enough to enter/exit?
3. **Best Bid / Best Ask:** moving up or down?
4. **Depth:** bids/asks being consumed, replenished, or pulled?
5. **Trades:** actual executions occurring at Bid or Ask?
6. **Price progress:** are those executions actually moving price?
7. **Absorption:** lots of execution but little price progress?
8. **Invalidation:** exactly what price action proves the trade idea wrong?
9. **Size:** calculate shares only after invalidation is defined.
10. **Order type:** Limit Order.

Golden rule:
**Depth = visible intent. Trades = actual execution. Price response = the verdict.**
